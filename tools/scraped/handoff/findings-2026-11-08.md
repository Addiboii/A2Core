## 1. Gathering system rework (user spec)

User: *"the gathering system is broken… make it as: first finishes the minigame animation with fail/not-fail percentage, then the amount can be gathered (3 line), then the gathering skills apply percentage to get a better item (white/green/blue/purple/gold)."*

Build gathering as **three sequential stages** per node interaction:

**Stage 1 — Minigame / success check.**
- Play the gather minigame animation, then roll **success vs fail** against a success chance.
- `successChance = baseNodeSuccess + gatheringSkillLevel * perLevelBonus − nodeTierPenalty` (clamp 5%–100%).
- On fail: node not consumed OR consumed with no yield (match official — fail just wastes the attempt, play fail anim). On success: go to Stage 2.

**Stage 2 — Amount gathered (the "3 lines").**
- On success, roll the **quantity**: 3 outcome lines (low / normal / bonus), e.g. weighted {1×:55%, 2×:35%, 3×:10%}. Higher gathering skill shifts weight toward the higher lines.
- Show the 3-line amount result in the gather result UI.

**Stage 3 — Quality roll (item grade: white/green/blue/purple/gold).**
- Roll the **grade** of what you receive. Gathering skill level applies a percentage that improves the odds of a better grade.
- Grades map to AION colors: **white=Common, green=Rare, blue=Heroic, purple=Unique, gold=Epic** (match the item grade enum already in the server).
- Base grade weights per node, then `gatheringSkillLevel` adds a shift (e.g. each skill level moves X% probability from lower grades up one tier). Cap so gold stays rare at low skill.

Tunables to expose (config): `baseNodeSuccess`, `perLevelBonus`, quantity line weights, grade base weights, per-level grade shift. Keep deterministic RNG seeding testable.

## 5. Fall damage fix (user report)

User: *"fall damage is delayed… should take damage based on fall distance (sometimes fatal) like real, and once hit the ground take damage, not after a few seconds."*

Two bugs:
1. **Timing** — damage lands a few seconds AFTER grounding. Apply it the instant the player becomes grounded. It's probably on a movement/physics tick, a deferred timer, or a coroutine — move it into the landing/ground-contact transition handler and apply synchronously (fall→grounded), not on a later tick.
2. **Amount** — base on actual fall distance, allow fatal:
   - Track apex Y while airborne; `fallDistance = apexY − landY`.
   - `safeFall` threshold → no damage below it.
   - Above: `damagePct = (fallDistance − safeFall) * pctPerUnit`, clamp 0..100, damage = `maxHp * damagePct/100` (percent of max HP so it's fair at all levels).
   - `lethalFall` threshold → damage = current HP (fatal); high falls must be able to kill.
   - Reset apex on new fall; **gliding must NOT accumulate fall distance** (only free-fall counts) — ties into the earlier glide work.
   - Expose `safeFall`, `pctPerUnit`, `lethalFall` as config; log fallDistance + damage.
   - Verify: short hop = no damage; big drop = heavy damage on impact (not seconds later); very high fall = death.
